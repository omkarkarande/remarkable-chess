# Chess-piece assets

The 12 chess-piece SVG sources in `ui/pieces/` were downloaded from the
[`lichess-org/lila` CBurnett set](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/cburnett)
and rasterized locally to 512px PNGs for AppLoad's resource bundle.

The Lichess repository distributes these assets under GPL-2.0-or-later.
